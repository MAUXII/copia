# Credits and removal

Most components in Copia are recreations of interfaces other people made
first. We do not claim to be the original creators. This is our attempt to
reverse engineer, replicate and often add a few features.

## How we credit

- `meta.json` → `inspiration` names the original creator and links the
  original. The component page shows it as "Inspired by …".
- The case study in `knowledge/case-studies/<slug>.md` lists every version
  studied (for example Skiper UI, Motion Primitives) with links.
- Demos use generated content: no third-party images, fonts or logos ship
  with a component.

## Asking for a change or removal

If you created something recreated here and want the credit changed or the
component removed, open a
[removal request](https://github.com/MAUXII/copia/issues/new?template=removal-request.yml).
We respond within a week, and we fix the credit or remove the component.
